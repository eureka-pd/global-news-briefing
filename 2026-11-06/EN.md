## 1. Korean stocks close sharply lower; US selling deepens before the cutoff

## 2. Bank of England raises Bank Rate to 4%

**Facts:** The BoE raised its rate by 0.25 percentage points to 4%. The vote was 8–1, with the dissenter seeking a larger increase. It projects CPI inflation near 5% in mid-2027.

**Analysis:** Energy-driven inflation remains a constraint on monetary policy. A forecast is not an inflation outcome, and the next rate decision remains conditional on incoming evidence.

**Sources and times:** [BoE statement and minutes](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/monetary-policy-summary-and-minutes/2026/november-2026), published November 5, time not displayed; [Reuters via MarketScreener](https://ca.marketscreener.com/news/bank-of-england-delivers-first-rate-hike-in-three-years-gilt-yields-rise-ce785fdcdf8ff026), November 5, 12:25 UTC / 21:25 KST, updated 14:39 UTC / 23:39 KST.

## 3. South Korea proposes expanding Cheonghae's operating area

**Facts:** Seoul plans to extend the naval unit's operations toward waters near Oman's Duqm port and seek parliamentary approval to renew its deployment beyond 2026. A ministry official described the mission as noncombat and independent of allied forces.

**Analysis:** The proposal addresses shipping security while limiting exposure to the Iran conflict. It is not an approved deployment inside the Strait of Hormuz; operations there would require separate conditions and approval.

**Sources and times:** [Yonhap](https://en.yna.co.kr/view/AEN20261105000700315), November 5, 09:30 KST / 00:30 UTC; [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/south-korea-expand-anti-piracy-unit-operations-closer-strait-hormuz-2026-11-05/), November 5, 01:54:41 UTC / 10:54:41 KST.

## 4. OpenAI president says enterprise now supplies most revenue

**Facts:** Greg Brockman said enterprise customers now account for most OpenAI revenue and expressed confidence in readiness for a 2027 IPO.

**Analysis:** Enterprise adoption is becoming more important to the business model. These are management statements, not independently audited results or confirmation of an IPO date.

**Sources and times:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/openai-ready-ipo-2027-now-counts-majority-sales-enterprises-brockman-says-2026-11-05/), November 5, 02:05:19 UTC / 11:05:19 KST; [Reuters via The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/openai-ready-to-ipo-in-2027-now-counts-majority-of-sales-from-enterprises-greg-brockman-says/articleshow/135556244.cms), updated November 5, 08:00:06 IST / 02:30:06 UTC. The remarks were made November 4 in California.

## 5. Qualcomm's handset weakness contrasts with automotive growth

**Facts:** Fiscal Q4 handset revenue fell 26% year over year to $5.137 billion, while automotive revenue rose 89% to $1.990 billion. Total GAAP revenue fell 8% to $10.322 billion.

**Analysis:** Diversification is progressing, but growth outside smartphones has not yet offset the decline in total quarterly revenue. This company result does not measure demand across the entire semiconductor industry.

**Sources and times:** [Qualcomm earnings release filed with the SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/804328/000080432826000127/qcom092726erex991.htm), November 4, after the US market close; [Reuters via AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/qualcomm-forecasts-upbeat-sales-strength-210438000.html), updated November 4, 23:39 UTC / November 5, 08:39 KST. Fiscal quarter ended September 27, 2026.

## 6. Korea builds a channel for foreign data-center investment

**Facts:** The Korea Data Center Council and KOTRA announced an investment-cooperation MOU signed November 4. They plan investor matching and assistance with power and permitting obstacles.

**Analysis:** This is a support framework. It does not establish that funding has been secured or a new data center approved.

**Source and time:** [Edaily](https://www2.edaily.co.kr/News/Read?mediaCodeNo=257&newsId=01931926645642768), published and updated November 5, 06:00:11 KST / November 4, 21:00:11 UTC.
