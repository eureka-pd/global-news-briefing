   - At 09:50, GeekNews featured [Patreon's notification-system scaling case study](https://news.hada.io/topic?id=31515). 4. **Chinese President Xi Jinping named open-source AI, international governance and support for developing countries as key pillars of AI cooperation.**
   - At 20:18, AI Times reported on his [opening speech at Shanghai WAIC](https://www.aitimes.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=41028). According to the article, Xi described AI as both a major opportunity and a new governance challenge, and stressed the launch of a World AI Cooperation Organization and cooperation on international norms.

### Markets and economy

2. **The semiconductor selloff spread across Asia, Europe and the United States, while Middle East tensions lifted oil prices.**
   - According to Reuters' [global markets roundup](https://live.euronext.com/en/financial-news/world-stocks-fall-semiconductor-rout-oil-rises-middle-east-escalation), published at 00:45 KST on July 18, Taiwan stocks fell more than 6%, the Nikkei 4% and MSCI Asia Pacific ex-Japan 2.7%.    - The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics released its [June Import and Export Price Indexes](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/ximpim_07172026.htm) at 21:30 KST. Fuel import prices fell 0.4%, but nonfuel import prices rose 0.4%, and import prices from China rose 0.9% from the prior month. Export prices fell 0.6%.

5. **A South Korean crude-oil carrier that bypassed Hormuz made the 14th Red Sea passage.**
   - At 11:45 KST, Yonhap reported on the [South Korean tanker's Red Sea passage](https://en.yna.co.kr/view/AEN20260717001900320). According to the Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries, the vessel was returning after loading crude at Yanbu, Saudi Arabia. With the Strait of Hormuz still unstable, the government considers the Red Sea route the most practical alternative.

- **Competition in AI technology and repricing in AI stocks are proceeding at the same time.** Very large open models such as Kimi K3 show that practical AI capabilities and investment continue to expand, while the same day's sharp semiconductor selloff shows that strong industry-growth expectations do not automatically justify current valuations.

If sustained, this combination could weigh on both rate-cut expectations and corporate margins, though one day's market moves cannot establish a long-term trend.

At the same time, as the war expands to ports, desalination and maritime traffic, voyage distance, insurance premiums, freight rates and import-price pressures require continued monitoring.
- **Competition over norms around AI and defense supply chains is intensifying.** China put open source and global AI governance on the agenda, while the EU targeted Russia's drone-component network with sanctions. Together, these events show that technology standards and supply-chain controls are becoming core instruments of diplomacy and security policy.
