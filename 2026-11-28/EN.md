# 2026-11-28 Global News Briefing

Reporting window: 2026-11-27 04:00–2026-11-28 04:00 KST (2026-11-26 19:00–2026-11-27 19:00 UTC)

## 1. KOSPI rebounds 4.34%; KOSDAQ jumps 8.13%
**Fact:** On Nov 27 the KOSPI closed at 4,915.68 and the KOSDAQ at 708.99. A buy-side sidecar briefly paused program buy orders on KOSDAQ during the surge. [1]

**Analysis:** Markets cited expectations of a stabilization fund. That is not evidence of actual disbursement.

## 2. Wall Street rises in shortened Black Friday session
**Fact:** The S&P 500 closed at 7,297.49 (+1.6%), the Dow at 50,774.94 (+1.2%) and the Nasdaq Composite at 24,129.50 (+2.3%) on Nov 27. The NYSE core session closed early at 1 p.m. Eastern. [2]

**Analysis:** The S&P 500 gained 5.6% on the week. That caps a rebound after large swings, not proof that volatility has passed.

## 3. EU puts forward next ECB president candidate; Ukraine loan back in December
**Fact:** The European Council put forward Pablo Hernández de Cos to head the ECB. Council President António Costa said 24 member states supported the additional Ukraine loan proposal, which will be revisited in December. There was no long-term budget agreement. [3]

**Analysis:** The nomination still requires consultation with the European Parliament and the ECB before a formal decision. The loan has not been finally approved.

## 4. US military says it disabled another merchant ship during Iran port blockade
**Fact:** AP reported that the US military said on Nov 27 it had disabled the propulsion of a merchant ship in the Gulf of Oman trying to breach the blockade of Iranian ports. Pakistani, Saudi and Turkish officials discussed regional security in Islamabad. [4]

**Analysis:** The ship account is attributed to the US military. The developments do not establish that the entire shipping route is closed.

## 5. Kioxia and Nvidia demonstrate SSD-backed GPU memory extension
**Fact:** Kioxia Europe said on Nov 27 that a GB200 NVL72 rack with 144 NVMe SSDs had about 1.1 PB of raw storage. The Nvidia SCADA proof of concept indicated potential to handle about two trillion 512-byte vectors. [5]

**Analysis:** The 1.1 PB is SSD storage, not installed HBM. The test does not establish commercial availability or HBM-class performance.

## 6. GeekNews: Google changes how AI agents access Workspace
**Fact:** GeekNews covered Google's Nov 24 announcement. Google says AI agents accessing Workspace must use its MCP servers or CLI and technical enforcement starts Feb 1, 2027. [6]

**Analysis:** Agent developers should review features that depend on direct REST access. This does not mean REST APIs for traditional apps are universally ending.

GeekNews was cross-checked with the original announcement. AI Times (aitimes.kr) returned HTTP 403.
