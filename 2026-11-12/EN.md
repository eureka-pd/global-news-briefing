## 1. Korean exports more than double in early November
**Provisional data:** Korea Customs Service figures reported by Newsis put November 1–10 exports at $33.46 billion, up 111.9% year over year. Semiconductor exports rose 331.2% and represented 49.7% of exports. The trade surplus was $8.75 billion.

**Analysis:** Semiconductor concentration matters as much as headline growth. Ten-day provisional figures do not establish the full-month trend.

**Source and time:** [Newsis, citing Korea Customs Service](https://nwww.newsis.com/view/NISX20261111_0003863187), November 11, 09:23:35 KST = 00:23:35 UTC. Growth rates refer to total exports and semiconductor exports separately.

**Analysis:** Strong demand and profitability are different questions. Guidance is a company forecast, not a completed result.
