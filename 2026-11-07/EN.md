## 2. US October payrolls rise by 165,000

**Facts:** The BLS reported 165,000 additional nonfarm jobs in October and unemployment of 4.1%. The Reuters economist poll had expected 65,000 jobs. These are initial monthly estimates, not a final count.

**Analysis:** Stronger hiring matters for the Fed's assessment of how much restraint the economy can withstand. It does not itself determine the next interest-rate decision.

**Sources and times:** [BLS employment release](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/empsit.nr0.htm), November 6 08:30 EST / 13:30 UTC / 22:30 KST; [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/view-strong-october-jobs-report-sends-yields-higher-2026-11-06/), November 6 13:49:11 UTC / 22:49:11 KST. The reference period is October, while publication falls inside this reporting window.

## 3. Democrats plan AI and data-center legislation

**Facts:** Reuters reports that US Democrats are preparing bills on AI safety testing, liability for AI-related harm, and data-center electricity costs for the next Congress.

**Analysis:** The scope of future rules matters to AI infrastructure investment, but proposals are not enacted regulation. Timing, final provisions and passage remain uncertain.

**Sources and times:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/democrats-planning-flood-ai-bills-when-they-take-control-congress-2026-11-06/), November 6 11:05:30 UTC / 20:05:30 KST; [Reuters via CNA](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/business/democrats-planning-flood-ai-bills-when-they-take-control-congress-6530926). The syndication is the same reporting, not independent corroboration.

## 4. EU issues preliminary TikTok findings

**Facts:** The European Commission provisionally found that TikTok inadequately addresses risks from recommendations repeatedly showing similar harmful content. TikTok disputes the findings and can respond before a final decision.

**Analysis:** This puts recommendation-system design under regulatory scrutiny. It is not a final infringement ruling or an imposed fine.

**Sources and times:** [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-preliminarily-finds-tiktoks-you-feed-breach-digital-services-act-harmful-rabbit-holes) and [The Record](https://therecord.media/eu-accuses-tiktok-of-rabbit-hole-DSA-violation), dated November 6, no publication time displayed; [Reuters via StreetInsider](https://new.streetinsider.com/Reuters/EU%2Bcharges%2BTikTok%2Bover%2Bharmful%2Balgorithmic%2Brabbit%2Bhole%2Beffects/27303013.html), November 6 06:02 EST / 11:02 UTC / 20:02 KST, confirming pre-cutoff coverage.

## 5. South Korean parliament passes special-prosecutor bill

**Facts:** The National Assembly approved a bill to investigate alleged fabricated investigations and prosecutions under the previous administration, including cases involving President Lee Jae-myung. The vote was 167–1 among 168 members present.

**Analysis:** Parliamentary passage advances the proposed investigation; it does not establish the allegations as facts. Findings must be distinguished from political claims.

**Sources and times:** [Yonhap](https://www.yna.co.kr/view/AKR20261106131151001), November 6 18:06 KST / 09:06 UTC; [Hankyung](https://www.hankyung.com/article/2026110614927), published November 6 17:24 KST / 08:24 UTC, updated 17:32 KST. The vote occurred November 6; its precise time was not verified.

## 6. China opens trade probes into EU and Japanese materials

**Facts:** China's commerce ministry opened anti-dumping investigations into EU para-aramid filament yarn and ethylidene norbornene (ENB) from the EU and Japan, effective November 6.

**Analysis:** The investigations add uncertainty for affected exporters and supply chains. Opening a case is not a finding of dumping or a decision to impose final duties.

**Sources and times:** [Chinese commerce ministry ENB notice](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zwgk/zcfb/art/2026/art_f587eac1695442ba86f2db6135b943b9.html), November 6 15:00 China time / 07:00 UTC / 16:00 KST; [ministry statement covering both investigations](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/xwfb/xwfyrth/art/2026/art_337d9e1ef6fb4138a1cdf1952597eca1.html), 15:23 China time / 07:23 UTC / 16:23 KST; [Yonhap](https://www.yna.co.kr/view/AKR20261106145900083), November 6 16:59 KST / 07:59 UTC. These are November 6 official actions, not merely plans.
