### 4. Apple moves EU apps onto one business model; per-install CTF becomes a 5% CTC

- **Source/time:** Apple Newsroom dated 2026-08-18; GeekNews curation at 21:28:47 KST on Aug. 19 — [Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/08/apple-announces-changes-for-apps-in-the-european-union/) · [Developer page](https://developer.apple.com/support/apps-in-the-eu/) · [GeekNews](https://news.hada.io/topic?id=32660)
- **Source facts:** Apple said that after work with the European Commission it is resolving disagreements on EU business terms and alternative distribution and moving every EU-distributing developer onto one set of terms. Developers can sign now; changes take effect Oct. 1. The per-install Core Technology Fee for very large developers is replaced by a 5% Core Technology Commission on digital transactions outside the App Store; the initial acquisition fee and store services fee go away. Rates: App Store + Apple IAP 26% (15% for Small Business, Mini Apps and Video Partner programs and for auto-renewing subscriptions after year one); in-app alternative payments 20% (10% in those programs); link-out 15% (10%); alternative marketplaces or web 5% CTC. IAP and alternative payments may be offered together, but the chosen mix must be kept for 12 months. Kids-category apps may not link out for checkout; under-18 users need a parental gate; under-13 apps cannot link out. Eligibility to run a marketplace or web-distribute expands (Dun & Bradstreet stability, public listing or public parent, venture funding, a licensed audit, or government/education/nonprofit), but notarization remains.
- **Analysis:** This is a company notice, not an independent DMA ruling. The rates apply from Oct. 1, not immediately. Reports that the Commission will monitor implementation are not a joint communiqué.
