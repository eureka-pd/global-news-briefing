## Analysis

- **AI competition is broadening from model quality to workflow economics.** GPT-5.6 efficiency, ChatGPT Work's artifact creation and industrial partnerships suggest that end-to-end execution and deployment cost are becoming key differentiators.
- **Korea's rebound remains exposed to both AI sentiment and geopolitics.** Strong SK hynix ADR demand and chip gains support risk appetite, but renewed U.S.-Iran fighting could lift oil and inflation risk, pressuring technology valuations and the won.
- **Military uncertainty on the Korean Peninsula increased despite separate diplomatic efforts.** North Korea disclosed no exact scale for the buildup, so subsequent tests and deployment evidence will be needed to assess the actual change.
