## Top developments

### 1. Korean exports set a record as equities rebound

- **Facts:** Provisional September exports reached **US$120.94bn (+83.5% year on year)**, including about **US$60.3bn** of semiconductors. The October 1 **KOSPI closed at 6,971.35 (+1.95%)**, ending three losing sessions; **KOSDAQ closed at 894.29 (+4.48%)**.
- **Analysis:** Chip demand supports the earnings narrative, but export value growth is not the same as shipment-volume growth or a broad domestic recovery. One positive session does not establish a lasting equity turnaround.
- **Sources/time:** [Industry ministry release](https://m.korea.kr/briefing/pressReleaseView.do?newsId=156783912), October 1, clock time not displayed; [Chosun Biz](https://biz.chosun.com/policy/policy_sub/2026/10/01/MBPGVYZZY5EFFGZRYKF63J3GII/?outputType=amp), October 1 09:00 KST, updated 09:47; [Yonhap Infomax](https://news.einfomax.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=4437413), 15:48 KST, updated 15:49; [YTN](https://www.ytn.co.kr/_cs/_ln_0102_202610011631497912_005.html), 16:31 KST.

### 2. Global bond yields remain a headwind for stocks

- **Facts:** At the September 30 US close, the **Dow was 50,906.05 (−0.86%)**, **S&P 500 7,651.54 (−0.25%)**, and **Nasdaq Composite 26,861.06 (+0.24%)**. On October 1, Reuters reported the US 10-year Treasury yield reaching **5.3445%** and the UK 30-year gilt yield **6.029%** during trading.
- **Analysis:** Higher long-term borrowing costs can offset earnings optimism and pressure equity valuations. The October 1 yields are session observations, not closing yields; the October 1 US stock-market close lies outside this briefing window.
- **Sources/time:** [MT Newswires, September 30 close](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/us-equity-indexes-mixed-treasury-211043319.html), September 30 17:10 EDT = October 1 06:10 KST; [Reuters, US morning](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/wall-street-dips-as-surging-treasury-yields-outweigh-software-gains-ce785ddad98cf024), October 1 11:00 EDT = October 2 00:00 KST; [Reuters, UK gilts](https://au.marketscreener.com/news/uk-30-year-gilt-yields-top-6-for-the-first-time-since-1998-ce785ad3df8af323), October 1 18:47 AEST = 17:47 KST.

### 3. US manufacturing expands while input-cost pressure rises

- **Facts:** September ISM manufacturing PMI was **54.5**, versus **54.6** in August; its prices index rose to **77.9 from 71.1**. Initial unemployment claims for the week ended September 26 were **197,000**, down **1,000** from the revised previous week.
- **Analysis:** Continued expansion and low layoffs coexist with stronger cost pressure, complicating the rate outlook. The ISM prices index is not a percentage inflation rate; advance claims can be revised. These releases do not settle the Federal Reserve's next decision.
- **Sources/time:** [ISM September report](https://www.ismworld.org/supply-management-news-and-reports/reports/ism-pmi-reports/pmi/september/); [ISM release schedule](https://www.ismworld.org/supply-management-news-and-reports/reports/ism-pmi-reports/pmi/august/), October 1 10:00 EDT = 23:00 KST; [US Labor Department release](https://www.dol.gov/sites/dolgov/files/OPA/newsreleases/ui-claims/20261543.pdf), October 1 08:30 EDT = 21:30 KST.

### 4. Micron reports record revenue and forecasts further growth

- **Facts:** Micron's fiscal Q4, ended September 3, produced **US$54.23bn revenue** and **US$33.42 adjusted diluted EPS (non-GAAP)**. Management forecasts fiscal Q1 2027 revenue of **US$61.5bn ± US$1.5bn**.
- **Analysis:** Actual memory revenue gives the AI-infrastructure theme a firmer earnings basis. The guidance is a forecast, and Micron's results cannot be treated as Samsung Electronics' or SK Hynix's future results.
- **Sources/time:** [Micron investor relations](https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2026/Micron-Technology-Inc--Reports-Record-Fiscal-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2026-Results/default.aspx); [timestamped company release](https://rss.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/30/3372366/14450/en/micron-technology-inc-reports-record-fiscal-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2026-results.html), September 30 16:01 EDT = October 1 05:01 KST.

### 5. Google introduces Gemini 4 Argon with restricted initial access

- **Facts:** Google announced **Gemini 4 Argon**, initially for trusted cyber defenders through Fairwind. Google says its output limit is **one million tokens**. No general-release date was specified.

### 6. Amazon's nuclear-power agreement supports additional capacity

- **Facts:** Amazon and Constellation announced a **20-year, 690MW agreement**, including **190MW** expected in **2030–2032**. Calvert Cliffs electricity stays on the PJM grid. The deal supports over **US$3bn** in infrastructure investment, not a disclosed contract price.
- **Analysis:** Cloud growth requires long-term electricity investment as well as chips. The announced additional capacity has a multi-year lead time and is not operating today.
- **Sources/time:** [Constellation release](https://investors.constellationenergy.com/news-releases/news-release-details/constellation-and-amazon-announce-20-year-power-purchase), September 30 16:00 EDT = October 1 05:00 KST; [Maryland Matters](https://marylandmatters.org/2026/09/30/calvert-cliffs-power-purchase-amazon/), September 30.
