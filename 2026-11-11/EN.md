## 1. US October inflation slows monthly but exceeds forecasts
**Data:** Seasonally adjusted CPI rose 0.4% month over month, down from September's 0.6%. Core CPI rose 0.3% for a third month. Reuters poll forecasts were 0.3% and 0.2%, respectively. Overall and core annual rates were unavailable because October 2025 survey data were missing.

**Analysis:** Monthly slowing and an upside forecast surprise occurred together. This release does not establish the Fed's next rate decision.

**Sources and times:** [BLS release](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.nr0.htm), November 10, 08:30 EST = 13:30 UTC = 22:30 KST; [Reuters / StreetInsider](https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/US%2Bconsumer%2Bprices%2Brise%2Babove%2Bexpectations%2Bin%2BOctober/27319611.html), 08:36 EST = 22:36 KST.

## 6. Google DeepMind publishes formal-verification research on Signal implementation
**Researchers' announcement:** AlphaProof Code combines human-reviewed specifications and AI proof agents to verify specific security properties of Signal's cryptographic implementation. The published technical paper excludes quantum adversaries and multi-device state, among other limitations, from its computational proof.

**Analysis:** Automated verification is becoming important alongside code generation. The result does not guarantee the safety of all of Signal or protection against every attack.

**Sources and times:** [DeepMind announcement](https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-alphaproof-code/), [technical paper](https://google-deepmind.github.io/code-verification/), November 10; exact publication times unverified.
