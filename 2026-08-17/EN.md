### 1. Apple asks a court to take 15% of App Store link-out purchases

- **Source/time:** GeekNews curation at 08:07:04 KST on Aug. 16; TechCrunch original at 07:54 PDT on Aug. 14 — [GeekNews](https://news.hada.io/topic?id=32541) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/14/apple-proposes-to-take-a-15-cut-of-purchases-made-outside-the-app-store/)
- **Source facts:** Apple told the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California it wants a 15% commission on iOS purchases made through external links, 5% for Small Business Program developers, and 10% for Video, News and Mini Apps partners plus subscription renewals. The Supreme Court refused to pause the lower-court process, forcing the filing. Apple says the fees recoup App Store tools, technology and services, and notes Google Play charges 20% on standard link-outs, rates Epic has accepted. The TechCrunch date may precede the window; the GeekNews timestamp falls inside it.
- **Analysis:** This is a proposal, not a final rate. The district court still sets a permissible fee while the Supreme Court case proceeds separately.

### 2. Deno publishes celld to self-host Cloudflare Durable Objects

- **Source/time:** GeekNews curation, 2026-08-16 09:30:02 KST — [GeekNews](https://news.hada.io/topic?id=32545) · [GitHub](https://github.com/denoland/celld)
- **Source facts:** GeekNews says Deno released celld, an open-source runtime that runs Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects on a user’s own machines. Each Durable Object is treated as a named cell with its own SQLite database, stored on user-owned S3-compatible storage or Google Cloud Storage. It supports HTTP plus Durable Object alarms, WebSockets and RPC; D1 and Workflows are planned, while KV, R2 and other managed Cloudflare services are out of scope. It is still alpha, so TLS, ingress and authentication must be configured by operators.
- **Analysis:** The project ports a programming model, not a finished Cloudflare substitute. Operational reliability still needs independent testing.
