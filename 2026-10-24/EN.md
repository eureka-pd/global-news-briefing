## 3. US consumer sentiment at 48.0; inflation expectations rise
**Facts:** The University of Michigan's final October sentiment index was 48.0, slightly below September's 48.1 but above October's preliminary 46.3. One-year inflation expectations rose from 4.6% to 4.7%; long-run expectations increased from 3.4% to 3.5%. The official release corroborated Reuters' figures.

**Analysis:** An improvement from the preliminary reading does not erase weak sentiment and persistent inflation concerns. Expected inflation is respondents' forecast, not the actual inflation rate.

**Sources and times:** [Reuters / Yahoo Finance Canada · 2026-10-23 23:19 GMT+9](https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/us-consumer-sentiment-near-record-141940652.html); [University of Michigan · 2026-10-23, time not stated](https://www.sca.isr.umich.edu/)

## 4. Eight-country Istanbul meeting stresses diplomacy and warns against escalation in Iran
**Facts:** Foreign ministers from Türkiye, Egypt, Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, Germany, France, Britain and Italy met in Istanbul on October 23 to discuss the Iran war, Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia and the Ukraine war. Turkish Foreign Minister Hakan Fidan warned that another attack on Iran would widen the regional war. Germany's foreign minister said Germany, France and Britain were ready for talks aimed at ruling out Iran acquiring nuclear weapons.

**Analysis:** This is multilateral diplomatic coordination, not a ceasefire or a negotiated settlement. Claims about future strikes based on separate reporting are excluded.

**Sources and times:** [Reuters / Yahoo Singapore · 2026-10-24 01:09 GMT+9](https://sg.news.yahoo.com/turkey-says-renewed-attack-iran-160903677.html); [Reuters / Internazionale · 2026-10-23](https://www.internazionale.it/ultime-notizie-reuters/2026/10/23/turkey-says-renewed-attack-on-iran-would-expand-conflict-diplomacy-only-way)

## 5. MiniMax confirms export registration for selected AI models
**Facts:** MiniMax said on October 23 that it had completed Chinese Ministry of Commerce technology-export registration for open-source releases and overseas licensing of some recently released and upcoming models. Reuters said DeepSeek had not immediately responded. AI Times Korea reported policy explanations distinguishing core-technology transfers from providing services overseas.

**Analysis:** The development suggests an effort to combine control over core technology transfers with international expansion of AI services. It is not unrestricted approval for all models or a new export-control law announced on October 23. The day's new development is MiniMax's confirmation.

**Sources and times:** [Reuters · 2026-10-23 15:59 GMT+9](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/chinas-deepseek-minimax-win-approval-ai-global-use-state-media-affiliated-entity-2026-10-23/); [인공지능신문 / AI Times Korea · 2026-10-23 17:06 KST*](https://www.aitimes.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=42412)
