## 2. Anthropic closes IPO and concurrent private placement
**Facts:** Anthropic’s November 20 statement put gross IPO proceeds at about $93.5 billion, including the overallotment option, and the concurrent private placement at about $26.0 billion. The $119.5 billion gross total before expenses includes sales by existing shareholders. [2]

## 3. Tankers hit in and near the Strait of Hormuz
**Facts:** AP reported two separate incidents logged by UK Maritime Trade Operations and another tanker fire reported by Iran’s navy. Tehran said it was still in touch with Qatari mediators, while President Trump called Iran’s terms unacceptable. [3]

**Analysis:** The mediation channel remains open but no new agreement was confirmed. Shipping risk persists on an important oil route.

## 4. COP31 runs into overtime over fossil fuels and adaptation finance
**Facts:** The UN climate talks in Antalya, Türkiye, went beyond their planned November 20 finish. AP and Reuters reports on November 21 described disputes over mentioning fossil fuels in the text and support for vulnerable countries adapting to climate change. [4]

**Analysis:** The reports describe negotiations in progress, not a final agreement.
