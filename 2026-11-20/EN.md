## 1. Seoul extends its rebound; Wall Street reverses intraday
**Fact:** On November 19 the KOSPI closed at 5,137.89 (+1.39%) and the KOSDAQ at 684.81 (+3.32%). In a Reuters report around noon New York time, the S&P 500 was down 0.77%, the Nasdaq Composite 0.58% and the Dow 1.29%. [1]

**Analysis:** U.S. indexes opened higher before reversing. The U.S. readings are intraday, not closing levels.

## 2. Anthropic starts trading 34.3% above its IPO price
**Fact:** Anthropic priced its offering at $83 a share. Its first Nasdaq trade on November 19 under ANT was at $111.50. [2]

**Analysis:** The opening trade is an early gauge of demand, not a close, a measure of profitability, or a broad AI-sector recovery.

## 3. South Korean exports cross $1 trillion for the first time
**Fact:** South Korea's industry ministry said preliminary year-to-date exports passed $1 trillion at 14:09:38 KST on November 19. Yonhap reported that semiconductors made up 42.7% of January–October exports. [3]

**Analysis:** This is the first time cumulative exports for a year have reached that milestone, not a final full-year total. Chips were a large share of exports through October.

## 4. Shenzhen U.S.–China–Russia summit commits to nuclear-risk talks
**Fact:** Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin met in Shenzhen on November 19. Their declaration covered U.N. reform, greater supply of refined petroleum products, and dialogue on nuclear risk reduction and nonproliferation. [4]

**Analysis:** The declaration calls for dialogue but sets no nuclear arsenal reduction target and announces no deal to end the war in Ukraine.

## 5. Google starts rolling out Gemini 4 Neon
**Fact:** Google announced Gemini 4 Neon for the Gemini API and Gemini for business, with rollout to paid Pro and Ultra subscribers in Antigravity and the Gemini app. GeekNews covered the announcement. [5]

**Analysis:** There are four thinking levels. Google's published evaluations do not establish performance in real workflows that use different tools.
