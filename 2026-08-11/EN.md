### 3. Korea plans a special law for semiconductor and AI megaprojects

- **Source and time:** Korea JoongAng Daily, 2026-08-10 21:34 KST — [Article](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/korea/korea-to-enact-special-law-speeding-semiconductor-ai-mega-projects/12817698)
