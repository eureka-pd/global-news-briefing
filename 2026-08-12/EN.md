## Key developments

### 1. Researchers disclose attack that extracts encrypted LLM reasoning traces

- **Source and time:** GeekNews, 2026-08-12 03:38 KST — [article](https://news.hada.io/topic?id=32413)
- **Source facts:** A [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09867) featured by GeekNews reports an attack that injects encrypted reasoning blocks into a weaker model from the same provider and induces it to reveal a stronger model’s reasoning in plaintext. The authors report demonstrations across Anthropic, OpenAI and Google models and claim recovery of 367 personal-data items and 182 credentials from 315,320 public reasoning blocks.
- **Analysis:** Publishing encrypted blocks in agent logs and cross-model compatibility may create a new leakage surface. The work has not yet been peer reviewed and needs independent reproduction and mitigation testing.

### 2. EPFL develops VirTues foundation model for spatial proteomics

- **Source and time:** AI Times Korea, 2026-08-11 17:26 KST — [article](https://www.aitimes.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=41397)
- **Source facts:** AI Times Korea reports that EPFL’s VirTues learns a shared representation from spatial-proteomics images using different marker panels. The [Nature paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10884-y) evaluates 32 cohorts, more than 5,100 patients and 239 markers, and reports stratification of treatment response and disease-free survival in triple-negative breast cancer.
- **Analysis:** The model offers a path to transferable spatial biomarkers across datasets, but retrospective cohort results do not establish clinical deployment.

### 3. Claude marks AI-generated content with text signals and C2PA

- **Source and time:** GeekNews, 2026-08-11 23:32 KST — [article](https://news.hada.io/topic?id=32409)
- **Source facts:** According to the [official Claude documentation](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/16266773-how-claude-marks-ai-generated-content) featured by GeekNews, supported models add machine-readable invisible signals to text and C2PA provenance metadata to supported image files. Detection indicates likely Claude processing, not complete authorship provenance.
