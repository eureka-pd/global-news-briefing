### 6. Go 1.27 ships with generic methods, encoding/json/v2 and ML-DSA

- **Source/time:** go.dev blog; GeekNews curation 08:36:37 KST on 2026-08-20 — [Go blog](https://go.dev/blog/go1.27) · [Release notes](https://go.dev/doc/go1.27) · [GeekNews](https://news.hada.io/topic?id=32677)
- **Source facts:** The Go team released Go 1.27. Language changes: generic methods; struct-literal keys that may be nested or embedded field selectors; function-type inference in all assignment contexts. `math/rand/v2.Rand` gains a generic `N` method for integer types. `go fix` adds atomictypes, embedlit, slicesbackward and unsafefuncs modernizers; `go doc` supports `package@version`; `go mod tidy` merges require blocks into direct and indirect. Allocation cost for objects under 80 bytes falls by up to about 30%, with about 1% overall on allocation-heavy programs, it said. The `goroutineleak` profile is generally available. `encoding/json/v2` and `encoding/json/jsontext` are added; existing `encoding/json` keeps compatibility while unmarshaling via v2. Also: `crypto/mldsa` (FIPS 204), `uuid`, experimental `simd`/`simd/archsimd`. Release notes restated the compatibility promise.
- **Analysis:** Speed and compatibility are project measurements, not a guarantee for every codebase. Stricter json v2 defaults can differ from old idioms. Experimental SIMD is not a stable API. The official tag may sit just before the window; GeekNews curation is inside.
