# 2026-11-26 Global News Briefing

Reporting window: 2026-11-25 04:00–2026-11-26 04:00 KST (2026-11-24 19:00–2026-11-25 19:00 UTC)

## 1. U.S. proposes considering tariffs of up to 15% on South Korean goods
**Facts:** USTR announced findings and proposed actions in 14 Section 301 investigations into manufacturing overcapacity. The Korean proposal contemplates a rate of up to 15%, net of most-favored-nation and existing Section 301 rates, with some exemptions. Comments are due January 5, 2027, and hearings are scheduled for January 25. [1]

**Analysis:** The measure moved from media reporting to a formal proposal. It is not a final, blanket 15-percentage-point surcharge.

## 2. KOSPI drops 6.16%; U.S. losses pare intraday
**Facts:** On November 25 the KOSPI closed at 4,697.51 (-6.16%) and KOSDAQ at 650.64 (-2.29%). Samsung Electronics fell 9.87% and SK Hynix 11.05%. At the November 24 U.S. close the S&P 500, Nasdaq and Dow moved -0.5%, -1.5% and +0.9%, respectively; at 13:55 Eastern on November 25 the moves were -0.2%, -0.9% and +0.7%. [2]

**Analysis:** The KOSPI erased the previous day’s rise. The November 25 U.S. readings are not closing prices; weakness in tech did not extend uniformly to all indexes.

## 3. U.S. October PCE prices rise 3.8% year on year
**Facts:** The BEA reported that October PCE prices rose 0.3% month on month and 3.8% year on year. Core prices excluding food and energy rose 0.3% and 3.3%, respectively. Real consumption slipped by less than 0.1% on the month. [3]

**Analysis:** Prices kept rising while real spending was nearly flat. The release alone does not determine the Fed’s next decision.

## 4. North Korea orders closure of Swiss diplomatic office
**Facts:** AP and Yonhap reported that North Korea ordered the closure of the Swiss office opened in Pyongyang last month and told its head to leave within 48 hours. Switzerland sanctioned the Songdowon camp over its alleged role in transfers of Ukrainian children from Russian-occupied territories. [4]

**Analysis:** Sanctions linked to the Ukraine war are affecting a newly reopened Western diplomatic channel. The order is not evidence that bilateral relations have been severed.

## 5. Microsoft launches internal ‘Code Red’ after November outage
**Facts:** Reuters, also covered by GeekNews, reported that Microsoft prioritized overhauling service reliability and recovery after an outage that lasted about 24 hours on November 12–13 and affected Exchange Online, Outlook and Teams. Microsoft said the incident was unrelated to cyber activity or AI agents. [5]

**Analysis:** The new development is the internal reliability effort, not a new outage or a demonstrated improvement in recovery.
