# 2026-11-19 Global News Briefing

Coverage window: 2026-11-18 04:00–2026-11-19 04:00 KST (2026-11-17 19:00–2026-11-18 19:00 UTC)

## 1. KOSPI regains 5,000; Wall Street slides intraday
**Fact:** On November 18 the KOSPI closed at 5,067.44 (+1.99%) and the KOSDAQ at 662.81 (+1.96%). At 11:32 a.m. New York time, the S&P 500 was down 0.48%, the Nasdaq Composite 0.08%, and the Dow 1.17%. [1]

**Analysis:** The KOSPI recovered 5,000 after a day below it. The S&P 500 and Nasdaq turned lower after opening higher. The U.S. close falls outside the window.

## 2. NVIDIA reports 103% quarterly revenue growth
**Fact:** NVIDIA reported FY2027 Q3 revenue of $115.8 billion (+103% year over year) and Data Center revenue of $107.6 billion (+110%). It said Vera Rubin NVL72 shipments had begun. [2]

**Analysis:** Q4 guidance is $130 billion (±3%), not reported revenue. The quarter includes an extra week and assumes no China Data Center compute revenue.

## 3. South Korea and China wrap up services and investment talks
**Fact:** On November 18 in Shenzhen, Presidents Lee Jae Myung and Xi Jinping welcomed the conclusion of follow-up negotiations on services and investment under their FTA. Seoul asked Beijing to help ease tensions on the Korean Peninsula. [3]

**Analysis:** Some technical wording remains and the new provisions are not in force. South Korea’s industry ministry says China agreed to nondiscriminatory advance reviews of online games and films. No resumption of U.S.–North Korea talks was agreed.

## 4. UK CPI inflation rises to 3.6%
**Fact:** The ONS put October annual CPI inflation at 3.6%, up from 3.5% in September. Core inflation changed little at 2.8%. [4]

**Analysis:** Motor fuels were a leading driver of the increase. The headline alone does not show a broad acceleration in prices.

## 5. Shenzhen diplomacy and separate preparations for Berlin talks
**Fact:** China confirmed President Trump’s arrival in Shenzhen. Axios, citing a U.S. official, reported a Trump–Putin bilateral meeting was added to the November 19 U.S.–China–Russia agenda; President Zelenskyy confirmed separate talks were expected in Berlin. [5]

**Analysis:** These are separate diplomatic tracks, not a confirmed deal to end the war in Ukraine.

## 6. GeekNews highlights how the Claude Code team verifies changes
**Fact:** On November 18, GeekNews covered the Claude Code team’s use of end-to-end, property-based and differential tests to check agent changes. The original post appeared on November 16. [6]

**Analysis:** This is an engineering case study, not a release. It shows how to gather evidence of actual behavior separately from writing the code.
