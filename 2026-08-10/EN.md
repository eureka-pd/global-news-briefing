## Key developments

### 1. Shopify moves inventory reservations from Redis to MySQL

- **Source and time:** GeekNews, 2026-08-10 00:33 KST — [Article](https://news.hada.io/topic?id=32303)
- **Source facts:** GeekNews highlighted Shopify’s migration of the inventory-reservation system that prevents overselling during checkout from Redis to MySQL 8. The design uses `SKIP LOCKED`, sharding and asynchronous cleanup; Shopify said it met throughput and accuracy targets during 2025 peak traffic.
- **Analysis:** Consolidating on a general-purpose database can reduce operating complexity, but the result depends more on contention-aware data modeling and load validation than on MySQL alone.

### 2. Alibaba Cloud opens the Wan 3.0 multimodal video beta

- **Source and time:** AI Times Korea, 2026-08-09 16:18 KST — [Article](https://www.aitimes.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=41358)
- **Source facts:** AI Times Korea reported that Alibaba Cloud began public testing of Wan 3.0, which accepts text, image, video and audio inputs and generates videos up to 30 seconds. The report says audio generation and video editing are integrated into the model.

### 3. KOSPI trading value and volume fall to yearly lows during correction

- **Source and time:** KBS World, 2026-08-09 12:41 KST — [Article](https://world.kbs.co.kr/service/news_view.htm?Seq_Code=203462&lang=e)
