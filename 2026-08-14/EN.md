### 1. Signal introduces automatic key verification

- **Source and time:** GeekNews, 2026-08-13 22:02:19 KST — [GeekNews](https://news.hada.io/topic?id=32469) · [Signal original](https://signal.org/blog/automatic-key-verification/)
