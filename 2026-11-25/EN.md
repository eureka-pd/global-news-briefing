# 2026-11-25 Global News Briefing

Reporting window: 2026-11-24 04:00–2026-11-25 04:00 KST (2026-11-23 19:00–2026-11-24 19:00 UTC)

## 1. KOSPI regains 5,000 as Wall Street opens lower
**Fact:** On Nov 24 the KOSPI closed up 6.32% at 5,006.03 and the KOSDAQ rose 4.79% to 665.88. At the US open, the Nasdaq Composite fell 1.43%, the S&P 500 0.90% and the Dow 0.20%. [1]

**Analysis:** Korean shares caught up with the previous US AI rally, which lost momentum in the next US session. The US figures are opening moves, not closing levels.

## 2. Korean regulators start joint monitoring of illegal short selling
**Fact:** The Financial Supervisory Service and Korea Exchange announced a joint team for three months of heightened monitoring on Nov 24. It will focus on steep price drops and spikes in short selling and investigate suspected violations such as naked short selling. [2]

**Analysis:** The Nov 22 plan has moved into implementation. This is not a blanket ban on legal short selling.

## 3. China extends suspension of extra US-bound export restrictions
**Fact:** China's commerce ministry extended its suspension of restrictions on US-bound dual-use gallium, germanium, antimony and superhard materials, as well as stricter graphite end-user and end-use checks, through Jan 27, 2027. The previous suspension ran through Nov 27. [3]

**Analysis:** The extension concerns the added US-specific measures. It does not abolish general export licensing.

## 4. New US tariffs remain at the reporting stage
**Fact:** Reuters, citing sources, reported that the US administration was preparing to release findings from an industrial excess-capacity investigation and propose fresh Section 301 tariffs. No official rates or effective date were verified by the cutoff. [4]

**Analysis:** The impact on trading partners, including South Korea, depends on the final products and rates.

## 5. Russian attack causes civilian casualties in Zaporizhzhia
**Fact:** AP, citing local officials, reported that a nighttime attack killed two civilians and wounded 48, including four children, in Zaporizhzhia. [5]

**Analysis:** Civilian harm continues despite US-mediated talks to end the war.
