# 2026-11-17 Global News Briefing

Coverage window: 2026-11-16 04:00–2026-11-17 04:00 KST (2026-11-15 19:00–2026-11-16 19:00 UTC)

## 1. Korean stocks slump; Wall Street falls intraday
**Fact:** On Nov 16, the KOSPI closed at 5,100.50 (-3.59%) and KOSDAQ at 665.65 (-4.86%). At Nov 17 03:37 KST, AP reported the S&P 500 down 1.9%, Nasdaq Composite down 2.4% and Dow down 1.4%. [1]

**Analysis:** Technology stocks weakened in both markets. The U.S. figures are intraday and not directly comparable to closing moves.

## 2. Alibaba releases Qwen4-Max
**Fact:** Following an earlier preview, Alibaba released Qwen4-Max on Nov 16. It says the model supports image and video input and up to one million tokens of context. [2]

**Analysis:** Smaller open-weight models are planned; Max weights were not announced as open. Cost and performance on sustained tasks still need independent testing.

## 3. COP31 calls for data center energy and water disclosure
**Fact:** The COP31 presidency and ITU launched the Antalya AI Pledge. Separately, Harvard researchers estimated about 25.7 million metric tons of CO₂ from electricity use at 403 U.S. hyperscale data centers from May 2024 through April 2025. [3]

**Analysis:** The pledge is voluntary and calls for clean power, energy and water efficiency, and disclosure. The modeled estimate is not a direct measurement for the entire AI industry.

## 4. U.S. tightens rules on remote access to advanced AI chips
**Fact:** The U.S. Bureau of Industry and Security announced an interim final rule covering remote access to advanced AI chips and servers through data centers in third countries. Reuters reported it on Nov 16. [4]

**Analysis:** This is not a blanket cloud ban. Operators should check due diligence and licensing requirements for the equipment, customer and transaction at issue.

## 5. South Korea proposes AI and creative-industry cooperation with ASEAN
**Fact:** At the Korea–ASEAN summit in Manila, President Lee Jae Myung proposed initiatives on AI and the cultural and creative industries. He said talks to upgrade the Korea–ASEAN FTA had begun. [5]

**Analysis:** Proposals and the opening of negotiations do not establish investment commitments or a revised agreement.

## 6. China confirms Shenzhen trilateral; Ukraine holds separate talks
**Fact:** China confirmed that Xi Jinping, Vladimir Putin and Donald Trump would meet in Shenzhen on Nov 19. Volodymyr Zelenskyy said he discussed prospects for ending the war with U.S. envoys and European representatives. [6]

**Analysis:** These are separate diplomatic developments. Neither announcement establishes a deal or ceasefire.
