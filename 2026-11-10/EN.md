## 1. Nvidia opens an independent inquiry into reports concerning its CEO
**Company statement:** Nvidia announced an independent inquiry into reports that Jensen Huang was targeted by foreign intelligence. The board expressed confidence in management and said it had no reason to believe national security or company secrets were compromised.

**Analysis:** The inquiry introduces governance uncertainty. Its announcement is not proof of espionage or a security breach; the outcome remains open.

**Source and time:** [Nvidia statement, distributed by GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/08/3398017/0/en/statement-from-nvidia-board-of-directors.html) · 2026-11-08 15:00 EST = 20:00 UTC = 2026-11-09 05:00 KST.

## 2. Korean AI infrastructure cooperation and startup investment plans advance
**Announcements:** Nvidia, Rebellions and SK Telecom announced NVLink Fusion cooperation involving Rebellions' R200 NPUs; deployment is expected in 2028. ## 3. China reports diverging consumer and producer inflation
**Data:** China's October CPI rose 0.7% year on year and core CPI 0.8%, while producer prices rose 5.7%. September's corresponding rates were 0.9%, 1.0% and 5.0%.

**Analysis:** Rising factory costs alongside softer consumer inflation suggest a squeeze on producers rather than broad demand strength. The figures are October observations released in November.

**Sources and times:** National Bureau of Statistics [CPI release](https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfbhjd/202611/t20261109_1965795.html), [interpretation](https://www.stats.gov.cn/zwfwck/sjfb/202611/t20261109_1965796.html) and [PPI release](https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202611/t20261109_1965794.html) · 2026-11-09 09:30 China time = 01:30 UTC = 10:30 KST; [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/chinas-october-producer-inflation-quickens-further-consumer-prices-edge-down-2026-11-09/) · first published 01:39:36 UTC = 10:39:36 KST.
