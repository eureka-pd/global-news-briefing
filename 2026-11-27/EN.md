# 2026-11-27 Global News Briefing

Reporting window: 2026-11-26 04:00–2026-11-27 04:00 KST (2026-11-25 19:00–2026-11-26 19:00 UTC)

## 1. Bank of Korea raises its policy rate to 3.25%
**Facts:** On November 26 the board raised the rate from 3.00% to 3.25%. Five members supported the increase and two preferred no change. It raised growth forecasts to 3.9% for 2026 and 4.1% for 2027 and lifted its 2027 consumer-inflation projection from 2.3% to 2.4%. [1]

**Analysis:** The bank expects inflation to stay above target for some time and still sees financial imbalances. It did not set a date for any further increase.

## 2. KOSPI gains 0.29%; U.S. stocks closed for Thanksgiving
**Facts:** The KOSPI reached 4,817.77 intraday on November 26 but closed at 4,711.21 (+0.29%). KOSDAQ closed at 655.71 (+0.78%). At the November 25 U.S. close, the S&P 500 and Nasdaq fell 0.3% and 0.6%, while the Dow rose 0.3%. U.S. cash equities were closed on November 26. [2]

**Analysis:** Korean shares recovered little of the previous day’s sell-off. The November 25 Wall Street figures are closes, unlike the intraday readings in the previous edition.

## 3. South Korea, Japan and China respond to U.S. tariff proposal
**Facts:** South Korea’s trade ministry said the USTR’s base proposal would bring covered duties on Korean goods to around 15%, accounting for existing tariffs; a conditional alternative of up to 25% could be considered if talks or commitments are unsatisfactory. Seoul disputes the structural-overcapacity finding. Japan and China also objected to the proposals. [3]

**Analysis:** The 15% figure is the base proposal and 25% is a conditional alternative. Neither is a newly effective rate; comments and a hearing are still ahead.

## 4. Zelenskyy warns of another attack on energy infrastructure
**Facts:** AP reported that on November 26 Ukraine’s president said Russia was preparing another major attack on energy, logistics and other critical facilities. Statements surrounding U.S. mediation continued. [4]

**Analysis:** This is an attributed warning, not confirmation by the cutoff that the attack took place or that a ceasefire was achieved.
