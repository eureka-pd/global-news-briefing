## 1. Microsoft publishes an agent-experience playbook
- **Fact:** Microsoft released an AX Practitioner Playbook and companion skill for evaluating agents, diagnosing failures and improving documentation and tools.
- **Analysis:** A practical framework for testing agent usability; vendor evaluation results should not be treated as independent validation.
- **Source / timing:** [Microsoft](https://developer.microsoft.com/blog/introducing-the-agent-experience-ax-practitioner-playbook/) — October 9, 2026; hour and timezone not verified.

## 2. Asana studies browser-agent cost optimization
- **Fact:** Asana describes 144 initial runs and 12 follow-up runs testing changes to browser-agent history and screenshots. Its optimized GPT-6.1 Sol configuration averaged $0.47 per run in the reported task.
- **Analysis:** Configuration can materially affect agent costs. This small, single-task study does not establish a universal model ranking or model-only speedup.
- **Source / timing:** [Asana original study](https://asana.com/inside-asana/cut-browsers-agent-cost), October 8; [OpenAI follow-on](https://openai.com/index/asana-browser-agent/), October 9. Publication hours unverified; original study may precede the window.

## 3. Whistle: local speech recognition resurfaces
- **Fact:** Cactus describes Whistle as a 16.9 MB, CPU-based speech-recognition package with seven supported languages; Korean is not among them.
- **Analysis:** Local processing may suit privacy-sensitive or offline use, but suitability and accuracy require testing. This is a community discovery, not an October 9 launch.
- **Source / timing:** [Cactus original announcement](https://cactuscompute.com/blog/whistle), October 2; [GeekNews item](https://news.hada.io/topic?id=35027) / [October 9 archive](https://news.hada.io/past?day=2026-10-09). Exact community posting hour unverified.

## 4. Korea expands the financial-hacking investigation
- **Fact:** Korean police said on October 9 that the dedicated financial-sector hacking team had expanded from 28 to 43 people on October 7, adding digital-forensics and international-cooperation staff.
- **Analysis:** Expanded investigative capacity is a response to the incidents, not proof that affected systems are secure or that attribution is settled.
- **Source / timing:** [MoneyToday](https://www.mt.co.kr/index.php/society/2026/10/09/2026100911371937599), October 9 11:46 KST; [YTN](https://www.ytn.co.kr/_ln/0103_202610091403333412), 14:03 KST. Action October 7; disclosure inside the window.

## 7. Oil falls as supply concerns ease
- **Fact:** Reuters reported Brent at $103.18 per barrel (-1.1%) and WTI at $91.00 (-0.5%) at 13:28 GMT. It linked the decline to Trump's Iran comments and prospective Chinese fuel exports. A separate source-based report said China approved about 3.7 million tonnes of October fuel exports.
- **Analysis:** Supply expectations can move prices before shipments occur. The Chinese approval was reported by sources, not confirmed by a ministry statement.
- **Source / timing:** [Reuters / MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/oil-falls-as-trump-comments-on-iran-talks-ease-supply-concerns-ce785ddfdf8af722), updated October 9 14:06 UTC; price observation 22:28 KST. ## 8. U.S. consumer sentiment weakens
- **Fact:** The preliminary October University of Michigan sentiment index fell to 46.3 from September's 48.1. One-year inflation expectations rose from 4.6% to 4.7%, according to Reuters.
- **Analysis:** Expectations are not measured inflation; this preliminary survey can be revised.
- **Source / timing:** [Reuters / Fidelity](https://www.fidelity.com/news/article/us-markets/202610091016RTRSNEWSCOMBINED_KBN3VP1LM-OUSBS_1) — October 9 10:16 EDT = 14:16 UTC = 23:16 KST.

## 9. Canadian employment declines again
- **Fact:** September employment fell by 68,300 following an August decline of 41,700. Unemployment rose from 6.4% to 6.5%.
- **Analysis:** Two monthly declines point to softer hiring conditions, but do not alone establish the broader economy's trajectory.
- **Source / timing:** [Statistics Canada](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/261009/dq261009a-eng.htm), October 9 release; [Reuters / AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/canadas-employment-surprisingly-shrinks-september-123323000.html), updated October 9 12:37 UTC = 21:37 KST. Data refer to September.
