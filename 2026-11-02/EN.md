## 1. Korean exports rise in October

**Fact:** Yonhap and Reuters, citing the industry ministry, reported October exports of $115.28 billion, up 93.7% year on year.

**Analysis:** Chip demand and working-day effects need separating; exports do not establish a broad domestic recovery.

**Sources and time:** [Yonhap](https://www.yna.co.kr/view/AKR20261101015300003?section=economy%2Fall) · 2026-11-01 09:16 KST; [Reuters/MarketScreener](https://uk.marketscreener.com/news/south-korea-exports-nearly-double-in-october-on-ai-investment-boom-ce785fd8da89f626) · 01:30 GMT = 10:30 KST.
