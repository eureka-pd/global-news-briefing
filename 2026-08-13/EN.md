### 1. KAIST study screens accounts for suspected foreign-linked influence activity

- **Source and time:** AI Times Korea, 2026-08-12 12:16 KST — [Article](https://www.aitimes.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=41406)
- **Source facts:** KAIST and Max Planck researchers presented an explainable-AI pipeline analyzing about 110 million Naver News comments from 2006–2025. It selected 23,998 of roughly four million accounts for expert review; this is not proof of foreign control.
- **Analysis:** The method may help narrow large-scale investigations, but its output should support human review rather than automatic sanctions.
