# 2026-11-24 Global News Briefing

Coverage window: 2026-11-23 04:00–2026-11-24 04:00 KST (2026-11-22 19:00–2026-11-23 19:00 UTC)

## 1. Jensen Huang returns to NVIDIA; U.S. stocks rebound intraday
**Fact:** NVIDIA’s board reinstated Huang as president, CEO and director. The SEC filing dates the decision to November 22; the filing and company announcement were public on November 23. At noon Eastern on November 23, the S&P 500 was up 4.9%, the Nasdaq Composite 6.8% and the Dow 3.2%. [1]

**Analysis:** AI-related stocks led the bounce. These were intraday readings, not closing levels, and the bounce does not establish a change in AI investment demand.

## 2. KOSPI falls 3.31%; regulator approves looser buyback caps
**Fact:** On November 23 the KOSPI closed at 4,708.54, down 3.31%, and the KOSDAQ at 635.45, down 1.58%. A sell sidecar briefly halted program sell orders in the main market. The FSC approved easier daily issuer buyback-order caps from November 24 through February 23, 2027. [2]

**Analysis:** The previous day’s plan is now a formal decision. It does not mean that stock-market stabilization fund money was deployed.

## 3. South Korean exports reach $77.4 billion on November 1–20; chips nearly half
**Fact:** Customs’ preliminary tally put exports up 101.5% year over year. Semiconductor shipments reached $38.5 billion, about 49.7% of the total. Both values were records for the comparable period. [3]

**Analysis:** Chips accounted for a large share of these exports. This is neither a full-month reading nor final data.

## 4. President Lee nominates five officials, including intelligence and foreign affairs chiefs
**Fact:** The Blue House named Kang Kum-sil for the National Intelligence Service, Lim Woong-soon for foreign affairs, Hong Ihk-pyo for unification, Han Jun-ho for interior and safety, and Kang Yu-jung for culture, sports and tourism. [4]

**Analysis:** These are nominations, not completed appointments. Kang Kum-sil would be the first woman to lead the NIS if appointed.

## 5. European Commission proposes 22nd Russia sanctions package
**Fact:** The Commission proposed extending transaction bans to about 60 Russian banks, targeting crypto payment routes used for circumvention and listing more than 70 additional shadow-fleet vessels. [5]

**Analysis:** The proposal targets financing and maritime transport together. The new package as a whole has not been adopted or entered into force.

## 6. Ukraine claims Benghazi strike on Russian warship
**Fact:** President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Ukraine’s security service carried out the strike on Russia’s Merkury in Benghazi, Libya, and claimed it sank. Libyan officials cited by AP described the vessel as badly damaged. [6]

**Analysis:** Ukraine has now publicly claimed the attack. Accounts differ on the vessel’s final condition; sinking is not independently confirmed.

## 7. VerifyLens 0.27.0 ties web PR verification to the diff
**Fact:** VerifyLens, covered on GeekNews, now withholds a Verified verdict on its web PR check if the evidence line lies outside the pull request’s changes. The rule does not apply to whole-feature checks in VS Code, JetBrains or CI. [7]

**Analysis:** The distinction helps avoid treating pre-existing code as proof that the current change met a requirement. The author’s own review is not an independent benchmark.
