- **Source/time:** Wiz blog, including a 19:57 UTC Aug. 17 update (04:57 KST on the 18th); GeekNews curation at 07:32:25 KST on Aug. 18 — [Wiz](https://www.wiz.io/blog/red-agent-snowflake-copilot-cicd-bug) · [GeekNews](https://news.hada.io/topic?id=32595)
- **Source facts:** Wiz said Red Agent found that `jira_issue.yml` in `snowflakedb/snowflake-connector-net` interpolated issue titles into a `run:` block, allowing command execution. The bug went live when PR #1218 was squash-merged on June 18, replacing a safe `env:` and `jq --arg` pattern with direct interpolation. After fixing a failed payload, Red Agent received a Jira token, email and URL and authenticated as `qa@snowflake.net` with read access across engineering, security-compliance and bug-bounty projects. A 19:57 UTC Aug. 17 update said Copilot reviewed the merged PR and called it clear, and that it is unclear whether the vulnerable change itself was AI-assisted. Snowflake said it investigated immediately and found no evidence of unauthorized access.
- **Analysis:** Disclosure followed the patch. Copilot co-authorship on the squash commit can reflect another file in the same PR, so it is not established that AI wrote the vulnerable lines. A missed static-analysis finding is not proof of industry-wide failure.
